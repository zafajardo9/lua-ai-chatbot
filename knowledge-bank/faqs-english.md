# Onboarding and Programs

## Study Resources and Access

### What is the system of the review?
All **100+ lecture videos** are accessible **24/7** from the start, allowing students to study at their own pace. Students can:

- Watch recorded lecture videos
- Take daily quizzes
- Answer exams
- Ask questions in the open chatline
- Reach out to instructors for help when needed

### Do you offer face-to-face (F2F) review sessions?
Currently, Kippap only offers an **online review**.

### Where can I find the Recommended Review Schedule?
You can find it in the **Additional Resources** section of the **Kippap LMS website** or the **Kippap Mobile App**.

### When does LMS access expire?
LMS access will expire **a day before the CE Board Exam dates**.

- For **April 2025 CELE**, the last access is **April 27, 2025**
- For **September 2025 CELE**, the last access is **September 26, 2025**

### What happens if I don’t finish my review?
You will lose access to the video and assessment materials.

## Program Flexibility and Changes

### Can I join the review even if I haven’t graduated yet?
Yes. Undergraduate students are welcome.

### I am a retaker and consider myself an average student. Is this review suitable for me?
Yes. The program is designed for all levels, starting with basic concepts before moving into more complex topics.

### I'm working. Is the review program suitable for me?
Yes. All lectures are pre-recorded and accessible anytime, so working students can study during weekends or at night.

## Enrollment Transfers and Re-enrollment

### Can I transfer my enrollment to the next batch?
Yes, if you are enrolled in the **Full Package**. For the **September 2025 Batch**, transfers are allowed until **March 2**.

### What does "transfer of enrollment" mean?
Your account access will be moved to the next batch. This means temporary loss of access until the next batch begins.

### What does "re-enrollment" mean?
You will need to register again after the current batch concludes to secure your slot for the next review batch.

### What does "extension of enrollment" mean?
It allows you to participate in both the current batch and the next batch.

Your student account will be deactivated at the end of the current review period. Access will resume when the next batch begins and will be deactivated again at the end of that batch.

### How can I re-enroll / transfer / extend my enrollment?
You must be a **Full Package enrollee**. Send an email to **kippapprograms@gmail.com**.

### Do you have Live Orientation?
There is no live orientation. Orientation videos are available in the **Kippap LMS website** and **Kippap Mobile App**.

### Do you have live lectures every day?
Daily live lectures are only available during the **Refresher Program**, which runs from **March 3 to April 4**.

There are also **Weekly Live Consultations** for Full Package enrollees every Saturday from **December 14 to February 22**, except **December 28**.

# Exams and Quizzes

### Do we have to install the Moodle App?
No. Only install the **Kippap app**.

### How can I access the videos and quizzes?
You must first complete the **Learner Profile** and then the **Diagnostic Exam**.

### Why is my Diagnostic Exam locked?
Because you need to complete the **Learner Profile** first.

### Are the exams and quizzes available to answer at any time?
Yes. Exams and quizzes are available anytime.

### Can the answers on the Diagnostic Exam be saved when you leave for a while?
Yes. Your answers will be saved.

### Is there a time limit for answering the exams and quizzes?
No. There is no time limit.

### Where can I find the solutions for the exams and quizzes?
You can find them in the **Overall Feedback** section on the Kippap LMS website after submission.

### How many attempts can I make on quizzes/exams?
You may retake quizzes as many times as you like, but only the **first attempt** will be recorded.

### Do pre-boards have a timer?
No. Pre-boards do not have a timer.
